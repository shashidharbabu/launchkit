**Turn 2 plan (7 of 10 judged; every cluster below recurred on 5 to 7 apps).** Ordered by spread times severity. One class fix per cluster, each with a test that can only pass or fail:
- **F1 profile gaps (7/7).** Understand writes read failures and website gaps to a separate `unverified` list; `gaps` holds product gaps only and may be empty. Profile UI shows both. Rotate `lk_understand` id.
- **F2 invented numbers and facts in posts and listing (7/7).** A deterministic provenance check: every number in a post or listing must appear in the profile, DNA, chosen pricing or listing sources; hard, so the repair pass names and removes it. Plus hard patterns for an invented origin ("I built X because/after/when"), a roadmap promise ("on the list", "on our roadmap", "coming soon") and unfilled "[Builder ...]" placeholders.
- **F3 pricing (7/7, five at 2).** The pricing recommendation starts from the app's current tiers (names, quotas, includes) and lists every change with current and proposed values; no tier, quota or feature outside pricing_current and the profile's features. The listing quotes only numbers from its sources. The stored `revenue_at` is recomputed from the tiers.
- **F4 competitor negativity (5/7, angles included).** A hard check: a competitor named in the profile (icp.current_alternatives) or pricing competitors within a sentence of a negative word (slow, paywalled, bloated, locked, closed, black box, expensive, and their forms); applied to posts and to brand angles.
- **F5 plan honesty (7/7 venue mismatch, 3/7 "ready" while incomplete).** The plan includes only selected venues and existing posts, lists missing or blocked posts, and is "ready" only when every included post is approved; a Reddit draft whose subreddit is not the selected one is flagged with a redraft for the selected subreddit.
- **F6 reel (7/7 approved with an invented mechanism or scenario numbers; clamped copy).** The script may use only numbers from the profile or site, chips and step three only in states the product shows; clamping cuts at a word boundary and a clamped slot blocks approval.
- **F7 signals (1 to 3 everywhere).** Reject threads older than 90 days (Exa and API dates), GitHub issues from repos under 20 stars or younger than 30 days, and authors promoting a tool; replies never claim "I built" and never criticise a competitor.
- **F8 deterministic defects.** `repaired` recorded only for fixes present in the final text; thread tweets checked per item; Show HN JSON failure (dub) retried with a no-scratch-fields instruction; the drive records a post as drafted only when its row exists; the provider-named rule matches AI providers only (Q4); Q1 approval during re-analysis.
- **F9 targets maturity (7/7).** An established product (maturity stage or stars) ranks release venues over launchpads; the rules summary says "unverified" only when no rules page was read.

- The harness (`run-eval.mjs`, `drive.rerun.mjs`, `eval-extract.py`, `eval-sheets.py`) takes `EVAL_OUT`, so a new measurement never overwrites earlier evidence.
- The judge brief is a file (`JUDGE.md`), fixed for the loop, so turn scores are comparable.
